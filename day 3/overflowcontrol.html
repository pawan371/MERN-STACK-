A paragraph is universally recognized by a new line break, which is often accompanied by an indented first line or a blank space between blocks of text. There is no definitive length requirement for a paragraph. In narrative or journalistic writing, a paragraph can be a single sentence long. However, a standard rule of thumb for effective formal writing is to aim for three to five sentences to balance thoroughness with reader clarity.
